ABILITY LINK

Master feature inventory for product development, MVP planning, investor/accelerator applications, and future 
roadmap 
Product vision 
Ability Link is an AI-powered accessibility infrastructure platform connecting people with disabilities to accessible 
healthcare, rehabilitation, employment, education, transport, travel and everyday services, while giving 
organizations tools to improve, verify and manage accessibility. 
1. User & Accessibility Profile 
 Account registration and secure login 
 Personal profile and optional disability/accessibility information 
 Accessibility needs profile: mobility, vision, hearing, cognitive, communication and other needs 
 Preferred language and communication method 
 Mobility-aid profile (wheelchair, walker, cane, etc.) 
 Accessibility Passport: portable profile of a user's accessibility requirements and preferences 
 Privacy controls for each accessibility attribute 
 Share Passport selectively with providers, employers or caregivers 
 Emergency contacts and important assistance preferences 
 Caregiver/family linked profiles and permissions 
 Profile verification options where appropriate 
 Profile completeness and accessibility-needs checklist

31. Recommended Long-Term Platform 
Ability Link should evolve from an accessibility discovery app into a B2B2C accessibility infrastructure platform: a 
trusted accessibility data layer, AI matching engine, Accessibility Passport, provider network, and 
enterprise/government toolkit that can scale across Europe and internationally.

Product differentiation 
 Personalized accessibility rather than a one-size-fits-all accessibility label 
 AI that understands individual accessibility requirements 
 Accessibility Passport that travels with the user 
 Combination of people, places, services and accessibility data 
 Community + verified provider data 
 B2B2C monetization 
 Accessibility intelligence for organizations 
 Global, multi-disability architecture 
Reference note 
This feature inventory incorporates the Ability Link concepts developed in our previous planning and adds 
product-level features that are useful for an accelerator-ready, scalable platform. Comparable accessibility 
products commonly use accessibility maps, structured ratings, barrier reports and personalized accessibility 
information; Ability Link is intended to combine these with an Accessibility Passport, AI matching and B2B 
infrastructure. Examples of comparable capabilities can be seen in current accessibility mapping and 
AI/accessibility products.
